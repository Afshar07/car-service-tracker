# Delegation

- The lead selects applicable context before delegating.
- Assign paths, constraints, acceptance criteria, and targeted verification.
- Workers do not scan unrelated context or modify unrelated files.
- The lead reviews worker changes and owns final correctness.
- Useful independent boundaries are browser OCR/pixel analysis and Android/Kotlin packaging. Shared mileage rules, storage contracts, and TypeScript/native snapshot decisions stay under lead ownership.
- Pass workers the applicable `.ai/README.md` route and already established findings; do not ask them to load every context document or repeat sufficient investigation.
- Keep worker edits within assigned paths; avoid concurrent changes to the route index or shared domain contract.
